How It Works

  1. Admin Setup: Admin logs in and registers employees with their details and departments.
  2. QR Generation: Admin generates unique daily QR codes for each employee automatically.
  3. Employee Check-in: Employees scan QR codes at the gate using their mobile devices.
  4. Real-time Validation: System verifies QR codes, location, holidays and leave status instantly.
  5. Automatic Reporting: Attendance marked in real-time and reports auto-updated with analytics.
